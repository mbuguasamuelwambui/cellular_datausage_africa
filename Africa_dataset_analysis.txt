1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Paper:   "Cellular Data Usage in Africa: a Case Study from Rwanda" (IMC 2016)
Authors: Sherif Akoush, Ahmed ElMezeini, Lucian Carata, Ripduman Sohan,
         Andy Hopper — University of Cambridge Computer Laboratory
Dataset: One week of anonymised traffic, Airtel Rwanda, February 2015,
         ~200,000 unique mobile subscribers

Two main directories:
  imc16/    LaTeX paper source + generated figure PDFs
  Africa/   Underlying measurement data (sheets/, raw .txt files, reference PDFs)

Folder structure:
  imc16/paper.tex                  Full conference paper (LaTeX source)
  imc16/paper-short.tex            6-page workshop version (LaTeX source)
  imc16/paper.pdf                  Compiled full paper (1.8 MB)
  imc16/fig/                       223 figure PDFs (~62 cited in paper)
  Africa/sheets/                   11 Excel workbooks (core data sources)
  Africa/*.txt                     19 raw tabular data files (up to 182 MB each)
  Africa/RTT_PDF/3G_in/            68 standalone hourly 3G RTT graphs (~1.4 MB total)
  Africa/ (41 PDFs)                Reference papers and hardware specs

Device landscape
  197,539 unique devices. 78% basic phones (voice/SMS-oriented), 15.6%
  smartphones (Android). Top brands: TECNO 42.8%, ITEL 34.6%, Samsung 3.8%.
  Both TECNO and ITEL are African-market feature phone specialists.
  Top models: ITEL IT5130 (23,736), TECNO T340 (23,490), TECNO T343 (12,540).
  Many devices 2G-only; subset with WCDMA/HSDPA (3G); smaller subset LTE.

Network quality
  LAN-side TCP RTT median ~200ms (high by global standards).
  WAN segment: sharp spike at exactly 200ms (49.8% of flows) — satellite
  backhaul signature. TCP out-of-order rate ~93% on 2G downlink (RLC-layer
  retransmissions appearing as TCP reordering). TCP retransmission rate ~4%.
  Outbound RTT: Rwanda-local ~6–15ms, UK ~165ms, US ~225ms.

Traffic composition
  Total: ~12.97 TB download, ~1.26 TB upload over one week. Ratio ~1:10.
  Google Video/YouTube: 20.5% of all download bytes (~2.66 TB).
  Facebook + WhatsApp combined: ~10% of both upload and download.
  xvideos: 4.5% of download bytes (4th by domain stem).
  Upload: facebook 6.5%, google 6.1%, googleapis 6.1%, dropbox 3.8%.

DNS behaviour
  152 million total DNS queries.
  ISP resolver (172.28.1.116): ~100M queries (dominant).
  Google 8.8.8.8: ~10M queries.
  ~80% of queries resolve in <100ms; heavy long tail.
  Chinese resolvers (Baidu) visible — Chinese-manufactured devices
  querying Chinese DNS by default.
  Smartphones generate far more DNS traffic than basic phones.

Content server geography
  29,888 destination IPs analysed with RTT-based geolocation.
  MaxMind GeoIP corrected where RTT evidence contradicts classification
  (several African-hosted IPs mis-classified as AS, corrected to AF).
  Google last-hop router: 209.85.247.69 in Kenya.
  EU PoPs (Amsterdam) also contribute significantly.
  CDN infrastructure sparse in-region; most content on long-haul links.

GTP-C session dynamics
  ~330K signalling messages in 20 minutes across 2 probes.
  High session churn consistent with a busy national gateway node.
  Probe 1 (SGSN-side, UMTS type 1): 75,420 messages, Update-heavy.
  Probe 2 (GGSN-side, UMTS type 2): 255,855 messages, Create-heavy.
  2,040 unmatched requests (~1.2%): sessions mid-flight at capture start.
